5. RF Placement Strategy

Key principle: GPS and LoRa in the quiet left zone, relay and buzzer in the noisy right zone, battery acts as a passive RF buffer between them. GND copper pour on both PCB layers provides shielding.

8. Assembly Notes

  • No drilling. All openings are formed by finger joint gaps — printed in place.
  • No supports. Both pieces print open-side-up. All features are vertical extrusions.
  • Mount PCB on 4x standoffs in base (press-fit or self-tap M3 screws)
  • Plug LoRa module (RFM95W) into J4 header. Route antenna wire along left wall interior.
  • Stick GPS module (BN-220) to lid interior with VHB tape, front-left corner. Cable to J8.
  • Drop 18650 cell into cradle. Thread 2x zip ties through floor channels, cinch over cell.
  • Solder battery wires (20AWG, 50mm) from cell to BT1 pads on PCB
  • Route power wires (12V, relay) through back wall openings
  • Press lid down — fingers interleave with base, alignment pins register position
  • Optional: seal finger joint seams with silicone for weather resistance

For weather sealing: apply a thin bead of silicone to finger joint faces before closing. The tight finger mesh plus silicone provides good water resistance without gaskets.
